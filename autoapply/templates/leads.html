Roles from your job-alert emails, best fit first. Star the ones that feel right; you don't have to look at all of them.
How leads work

After each email sync, the top leads are looked up on the company's own job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable; no AI). Matches join the roles the daily run can tailor. Star a lead and the next run searches the web for it; Auto apply does that for one role right away and uses credits. Score is a rough fit from your titles and resume; point at a score to see what made it up.
