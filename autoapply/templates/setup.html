Finish applications in the browser{{ 'on' if finish_on else 'optional' }}

When a site asks for a captcha or a code, jobsearchPal can open the filled-in form in a browser on this server, and you finish it from the job page in whatever browser you're using, even on a phone. Nothing is installed where you sit.

That browser visits the job site through a proxy that can't reach your home network, keeps nothing after it closes, and never presses Submit. It needs Docker on this server.
